1. Wall photo

Take or upload a straight-on photo of the wall. Uploading a new photo clears any marked holds.

2. Mark holds

Sample: tap a hold to auto-detect every hold of that color. Add: tap to manually drop a marker. Edit: tap a marker to select it, then drag, resize, or delete. Route: tap markers to toggle them into the active route.
